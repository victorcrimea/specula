Assets for the 3D view


To show the match in 3D, this observer uses the game's own models,
textures, animations and sounds. It downloads them from this server as the
match needs them and keeps them in your browser's storage. Your next match
or visit then reuses them instead of downloading them again.


Over time this observer may store up to {size} of data on your
machine.


You don't need to wait for any of it. The match starts right away, and
each model is fetched the first time it comes on screen. It shows first as
a small rough version of a few KB, and full detail follows. A lower texture
quality in the settings stores less.


To load fast on any device, the graphics start at their minimum. You can change the graphics settings at any time in the settings.


Warm up downloads, in the background, everything this match can
need: the buildings and units of every civilisation in it, the map's
terrain, and the trees, animals and rocks on it. Then nothing has to load
when it first appears. This helps on a slow connection, or before the
armies meet. It also asks your browser to keep these files rather than
clear them when space runs low, and some browsers will ask your permission
first.


Press F4, or tap with four fingers, to cycle through the debug
information: network, profiling and storage statistics.


You can delete the stored files at any time in your browser's site data
settings.
